Privacy Policy

App: Chhath Countdown  ·  Developer: Rakesh Ranjan  ·  Last updated: 25 September 2026

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, and it does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Data kept on your device

Your chosen district and reminder settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Internet use

The app downloads the daily song list from our website and plays videos using YouTube's embedded player. When you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads and payments

The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
