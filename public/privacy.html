Privacy Policy

App: Chhath Countdown  ·  Developer: Rakesh Ranjan  ·  Last updated: 25 September 2026

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, and it does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Data kept on your device

Your chosen district, language, reminder settings, any profiles you create (a name and number of soops), your samagri checklist, and a WhatsApp group link you pin on the Live screen are stored only on your phone. The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste on the Live screen. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Profiles are not accounts: there is no sign-up, password or server. Deleting a profile, or uninstalling the app, removes this data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's approximate location to pick the nearest district or city for sunrise and sunset times. This happens on your phone; your location is never stored on a server or sent to us or anyone else. You can say no, turn it off in your phone's settings, or choose your district by hand at any time.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Internet use

The app downloads the daily song list and a list of live broadcast links from our website, and plays videos using YouTube's embedded player. The Live and Share buttons open other apps (such as WhatsApp, Google Meet, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook). The app cannot see your accounts, contacts or groups in those apps; their own privacy policies apply there. When you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads and payments

The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
